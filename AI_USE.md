# AI assistance and responsibility

OpenAI Codex assisted literature retrieval, extraction and comparison of source
material, statistical analysis design, code writing and checking, figure
preparation, drafting, and revision. Anthropic Claude provided an adversarial
review of the draft. PatAgent supplied evidence-linked retrieval and
methodological checklists. The author directed the review, assessed suggestions,
revised the manuscript, and is responsible for the submitted work.

The reported secondary results were calculated by Python scripts using released
annotations or published aggregate counts. No new benchmark model runs, paid
model-API experiments, or AI relabeling of benchmark responses were performed.
This does not mean that source datasets were free of model judging: biological
RefusalBench uses released model-adjudicated labels, as the manuscript explains.

The release includes separate arithmetic/data-integrity checks and saved result
comparisons. These were also AI-assisted; they are not independent human expert
validation. Computational reproduction does not validate the original scoring,
sampling assumptions, or causal interpretation. Proposed experiments were not run.

PatAgent wrapper version 0.1.0 is frozen with file hashes in its source-provenance
record. Its checklist output is an aid, not scientific validation. PatAgent is
not Pat Walters, does not speak for him, and implies no endorsement.

The interaction record identifies the tools as Codex and Claude but does not
establish an exact underlying model snapshot for every assistance session. No
unverified model version is supplied. Assistance occurred during drafting and
review in October–November 2026. No AI system is listed as an author.
